OPERATIONAL AUDIT RULES — 2026

1. CURRENCY CONVERSION
Transactions recorded in EUR must be converted to USD
using the internal operational exchange rate:

1 EUR = 1.12 USD

Transactions already recorded in USD must not be converted.

2. HIGH-RISK CUSTOMER EXCLUSION
Customers classified as High risk in the customer registry
must be excluded from corporate financial liability summaries.

3. MISSING CUSTOMER ID
Transactions with a missing Customer ID must be excluded
from customer-level financial aggregation.

4. MISSING SALES
Transactions with a missing Sales value must not be included
in revenue calculations.

5. DUPLICATE RECORDS
Duplicate transaction records must be identified before
performing financial aggregation.

6. NEGATIVE QUANTITY
A negative Quantity value should be flagged as a data-quality
issue and should not automatically be treated as a valid sale.

7. AMBIGUOUS QUESTIONS
If a question does not clearly specify the required metric,
aggregation, time period, or interpretation, the system should
ask for clarification rather than making an unsupported
assumption.

8. INSUFFICIENT INFORMATION
If the available data does not contain the information required
to answer a question reliably, the system must refuse to provide
a numerical answer and explain what information is missing.
